TRUST & TRANSPARENCY

Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

EGER is committed to protecting student privacy, maintaining open educational transparency, and empowering learners with complete trust.

Effective Date: October 2026 • Platform Version: 1.2
🔒

1. Privacy Policy

At EGER ("The Future"), we believe educational resources should be accessible without sacrificing privacy. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information.

Information We Collect & How It's Handled

  • Theme Preference: We store your chosen viewing mode (Light or Dark) locally on your device via localStorage. This data never leaves your browser.
  • Interactive Calculators: The DSAT Study Plan Generator and Placement Predictor run entirely client-side inside your browser. No scores, GPA values, or academic goals are transmitted to an external server.
  • Community Waitlist Form: When you voluntarily register for the EGER Scholars waitlist, we collect your name, grade level, and contact method (Email or Telegram handle) exclusively to notify you of free webinars, mentorship cohorts, and college application workshops.

Third-Party Links & External Platforms

EGER references official portals, testing bodies, and content creators (including College Board, Khan Academy, Ethiopian Ministry of Education, Dan Academy, and YouTube). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content policies of third-party platforms.

Student Data Protection

We adhere to global youth data protection standards. We do not engage in behavioural tracking, ad retargeting, or data profiling.
